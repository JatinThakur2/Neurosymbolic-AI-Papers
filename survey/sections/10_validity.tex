\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:validity}

\textbf{LLM-based reviewers.} Every screening and coding decision was made by LLM agents under a written protocol. Independent double assessment gave substantial agreement ($\kappa=\cnt{kappaunion}$ for stage-1 screening, \cnt{kappanuunion} without records both screeners marked unsure; \cnt{kappafaith}--\cnt{kappaform} for coding), but two runs of models of the same family under the same prompt share errors, so agreement shows that the rules are applied consistently, not that they are applied correctly.
\ifhumancheck
The human check bounds this risk: \cnt{precision}\% (95\% CI \cnt{precisionlo}--\cnt{precisionhi}) of the pipeline's inclusions were confirmed, so about one in \cnt{precisioninv} included studies may fail the protocol, mostly at the boundary of what counts as an interacting symbolic component. All counts are therefore upper bounds; Section~\ref{sec:taxonomy-sens} reports the main proportions on the confirmed studies.
\ifsecondrater
Human--human agreement ($\kappa=\cnt{secscrkappa}$ for screening) provides the baseline against which these figures should be read.
\fi
\else
The human checks have been prepared but not yet carried out, so the size of any shared bias is unknown.
\fi
Model versions were not recorded for every batch, so the decisions cannot be regenerated, only audited.

\textbf{Search recall.} Estimated recall of the full search is \cnt{recalltwo} (95\% interval \cnt{recalltwolo}--\cnt{recalltwohi}): about half of the eligible studies in Source B are probably missing. The missed studies the audit found are mostly near the eligibility boundary and resemble the found ones in architecture mix, but they are too few to test whether shares or trends would change. Known-item recall on an independent survey's references was higher (\cnt{kifound} of \cnt{kielig}), but that survey covers a domain whose abstracts name their solvers and provers. Recall may also differ by year.

\textbf{Coverage.} Ten venues are covered, eight of them through Source B. AAAI 2020 and 2026, IJCAI 2025, TMLR and TPAMI are covered only by the title-keyword corpus, which under-represents them. For 2026, Source B holds only ICLR and ICML; ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, IJCAI and NeurIPS 2026 are absent. ACL Findings, other venues (CVPR, ICCV, CAV, IJCAR, ITP) and arXiv-only work are out of scope by design, so our conclusions describe these venues rather than the field as a whole; excluding Findings in particular under-represents NLP work.

\textbf{Construct validity.} The boundary of ``language model'' and ``symbolic component'' is set by rules R1--R6. Different rules would change the included set. Counting models trained from scratch on synthetic symbolic data, or code generation judged only by tests, would add many studies; removing program-aided reasoning would remove \cnt{Fthreen} studies, leave the association and the trends intact, but make exact checks the majority within A2 (Section~\ref{sec:taxonomy-sens}). The architecture shares depend on the decision-tree order, which was fixed after the pilot; the trends do not.

\textbf{Abstract-level charting.} Codes and entities come from titles and abstracts, with the full text consulted only in adjudication. The full-text sample shows that the exact/empirical split within A2 is reliable, but that abstracts under-report formalisation faithfulness by a wide margin; we therefore report faithfulness from full texts where it matters and keep it out of the headline findings. Entity counts describe reporting in abstracts, not usage, and miniF2F results are headline numbers without controlled budgets.

\textbf{Artifact estimate.} The web check covers a random sample of \cnt{artrandn} stage-1 studies and the stage-1 A7 census. \cnt{artrandunc} sampled studies remained unclear, and we did not test whether released code runs.

\textbf{Reliability and independence.} The analysis is deterministic from the released decision files and the source commits; random draws use fixed seeds. The protocol was not registered in advance and was amended twice, once after the pilot and once during revision; every amendment is listed. \ifanonymous One author\else The author\fi{} curates Source A, which is why the human check includes an independent second rater and why Source B, which is independent of the author, carries the search.
