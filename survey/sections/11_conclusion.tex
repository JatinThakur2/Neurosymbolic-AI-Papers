\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This scoping review mapped \cnt{included} studies that integrate language models with explicit symbolic components at ten top-tier AI and NLP venues. The search covered \cnt{sourceb} abstracts in two stages, and a double-screened audit puts its recall at about one half, so the map describes the retrievable part of the literature. Normalised by venue size the area grew sharply, but as a share of language-model research it changed little between 2021 and 2025. Seven integration architectures describe the field. Within venues, designs in which a symbolic checker gives verdicts on model outputs have gained ground over symbolic guidance of generation, under every coding order we tried, although most such checks are empirical rather than sound---a balance driven by program-aided designs. The evidence points to concrete priorities: measure formalisation faithfulness and report it prominently; replace saturating, defect-prone benchmarks with audited, versioned suites; turn empirical checks into exact ones where possible; and report results against a shared checklist. We release every decision, prompt and script, so that this map can be audited, corrected and extended.
