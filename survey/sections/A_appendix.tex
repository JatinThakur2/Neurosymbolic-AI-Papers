\section{A Reporting Checklist for Language-Model--Symbolic Systems}
\label{app:checklist}
The review shows that results in this area are hard to compare because studies report different things (Sections~\ref{sec:evaluation} and \ref{sec:challenges}). We propose the following minimal checklist. Each item corresponds to a gap we observed.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Architecture.} Which pattern (A1--A7 or a combination) the system uses, and where the symbolic component sits: before, inside or after generation, at training or at inference.
  \item \textbf{Models.} Model name, version or checkpoint, size, and whether it was fine-tuned; for proprietary models, the access date.
  \item \textbf{Symbolic engine.} Name and version of the solver, prover, planner or interpreter, and the library version (for example, the Mathlib commit).
  \item \textbf{Check strength.} Whether the symbolic side gives a sound verdict on the final output (exact), a test- or data-based check (empirical), or no check; and what can pass the check while being wrong.
  \item \textbf{Formalisation faithfulness.} When the model writes formal statements: how faithfulness was measured (gold formalisations, round-trip translation, equivalence checking, human judgement), on how many items, and the result, reported separately from end-task accuracy.
  \item \textbf{Budget.} Number of samples per problem, number of checker or solver calls, wall-clock time and, where possible, cost; the pass@$k$ definition used.
  \item \textbf{Benchmark version.} Benchmark name, split, version or commit, and known defects or exclusions applied (for example, corrected miniF2F statements).
  \item \textbf{Baselines.} A model-only baseline with the same budget, and a symbolic-only baseline where one exists.
  \item \textbf{Artifacts.} Links to code, prompts, formal statements produced by the model and checker logs.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Replication Package}
\label{app:replication}
\texttt{code/search/fetch\_sources.sh} retrieves the sources at fixed commits: the Neurosymbolic-AI-Papers corpus at \texttt{9a42645}, Paper Copilot \texttt{paperlists} at \texttt{9fcfd12} (ICLR 2025 and 2026 from blobs \texttt{d4c51fa} and \texttt{33f9884}) and the ACL Anthology at \texttt{73f3b3a}. It also reads the ACL Anthology main volumes of ACL 2021--2025, EMNLP 2021--2024 and NAACL 2021--2025 at the same commit. Running \texttt{python code/run\_all.py --sources <dir>} then regenerates every derived file, table and macro in this paper from the decision files. The package contains the following.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{data/decisions/}: every decision made by a reviewer, never written by the pipeline.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{protocol\_v2.md}: the protocol with its revision amendments.
    \item \texttt{screening/}: both screeners' decisions and all adjudications, with evidence, for the stage-1 union, stage 2, the snowball candidates, the recall audit and its extension, and the known-item check.
    \item \texttt{coding/}: both coders' charting of all included studies and the adjudications; the supplementary coding (contribution type and mechanisms).
    \item \texttt{knownitem/}: the reference list of the independent survey used for the known-item check.
    \item \texttt{fulltext\_check/}: the full-text codes of the validation sample and its sampling design.
    \item \texttt{webcheck/}: the artifact web check with evidence URLs and its sampling design.
    \item \texttt{results/}: the extracted miniF2F results with supporting quotes, and their comparability classification.
    \item \texttt{human\_check/}: the blinded sheets for the author's check and for the independent second rater.
    \item \texttt{v1\_pilot/}: the decisions of the pilot version, kept for comparison.
  \end{itemize}
  \item \texttt{docs/prompts/}: the task prompts given to every LLM reviewer.
  \item \texttt{data/derived/}: files generated by the pipeline, including \texttt{screening\_log.csv} (one row per record, with every decision), \texttt{included\_studies.csv}, \texttt{entities\_long.csv}, \texttt{sensitivity.json} and the plot data.
  \item \texttt{code/}: the search (\texttt{search/}: Source B, both query stages, snowballing, audit sampling, known-item matching), the pipeline stages s10--s16, \texttt{human\_check.py} and \texttt{run\_all.py}.
  \item \texttt{tables/}, \texttt{figures/}: generated \LaTeX{} fragments and the TikZ/pgfplots figure sources.
\end{itemize}
